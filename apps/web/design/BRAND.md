# Intellect: a home for curious minds

Frontend direction: the supplied social book marketplace brief, expressed through an original playful reading interface. Duolingo Design and Mascot skills informed the color, feedback, and character direction. This is independent artwork and does not represent Duolingo or Notion.

## Design system

- Primary: lime `#a5ed6e`, paired with dark green text.
- Friendly canvas: sky blue `#ddf4ff`, on off-white `#fbfcf9`.
- Essential text: dark green `#293d30` and charcoal `#3c4941`.
- Supporting colors: warm peach, lavender, mint, and butter yellow.
- Type: Nunito for rounded, expressive headings; DM Sans for readable UI.
- Spacing: 10px rhythm with 12px corners, larger radii for feature illustrations.
- Interaction: raised primary buttons, visible focus, text alongside success feedback, reduced-motion support.

## Pip

Pip is an original green book dragon with leaf-shaped ears, round charcoal glasses, a gentle smile, and an orange book. A calm, curious reading companion, not a replica of an existing mascot.

Asset: `public/images/pip-reading.webp` (640 × 640 with alpha), optimized from a built-in image generation result. Alt text: “Pip, a friendly green book dragon wearing glasses and reading an orange book.”

Generation prompt: One original square, transparent-background mascot for Intellect: a charming lime-green book dragon, round pear-shaped body, tiny leaf-shaped ears, round charcoal reading glasses, peach cheeks, sitting cross-legged holding an open cream book with an orange cover. Flat hand-drawn editorial illustration, gentle dark outlines, lime, deep green, charcoal, cream and orange; clear silhouette, minimal detail, no text or scenery, no existing brand character.

## Preview behavior

Book inventory, prices, reader counts, and discussion prompts are sample content. Saves, reading goals, private reflections, reader nickname, and draft book listings persist in browser local storage. No checkout, live audio, shared posting, shipping, or account services are implied to be connected. Book cover thumbnails are obtained from Open Library by ISBN; authors and books retain their respective rights.

Existing browser-local reading features and saved data are preserved. Collection cards navigate to category filters in the preview catalog.
